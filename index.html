yt-hochladen

Developed with YouTube · uses YouTube API Services

yt-hochladen is a private command-line tool used by the creator of the YouTube channel Afrobama (@afrobama_yt) to upload their own finished videos (YouTube Shorts) to their own YouTube channels.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

What it does

  • Uploads a local video file to a YouTube channel the signed-in owner manages, with title, description, tags, language, privacy status and an optional scheduled publish time (YouTube Data API v3, videos.insert).
  • Reads the signed-in account's own channel name and ID (channels.list, mine=true) so the owner can choose which of their channels a video goes to.

What it does not do

  • It is not a public service and has no other users. It runs only on the channel owner's own computer.
  • It does not read, download, analyse, store or share any other YouTube data, analytics, comments or data of other users.
  • It does not sell, share or commercialise any data.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
